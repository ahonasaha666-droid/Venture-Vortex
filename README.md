# 👓 EYECONIC

### *Frame Your World*

EYECONIC is a direct-to-consumer eyewear startup that lets people design and try on **fully customised frames** from home, using an AI-integrated, Bitmoji-inspired virtual try-on filter. Frames are made on demand by artisans, shipped straight to the customer, and can be returned for recycling in exchange for store credit.

> Built by **Team Catalyst** (VIT-AP) for the **Venture Vortex, Round 2** submission.

## ❗ The Problem

Eyewear offers very little room for **customization and creativity**. People who want a specific look (for a costume, an office shoot, or an everyday style they have pictured in their head) struggle to find the exact frame.

## 💡 The Solution

An **AI-integrated home try-on filter**, inspired by Bitmoji-style avatars, that helps users find the exact eyewear they are looking for, down to:

- **Material**
- **Texture**
- **Colour**
- **Design**
- **Frame accessories**: charms, chains and magnetic convertible brooches

## ✨ Key Features

- 🤳 **Virtual home try-on** with an AI filter
- 🎨 **Deep customization** of material, texture, colour, design and accessories
- 🎬 **Movies & dramas section**: get the exact frames from your favourite film or K-drama
- ♻️ **Recycling loop** with store credit points
- 🏭 **Made on demand** by artisans and delivered **direct to your door** (no middleman)
- 🔍 **Quality lenses** sourced from Crizal

## 💼 Business Model

| | |
|---|---|
| **Channel** | Direct-to-consumer (D2C): in-house factory and artisans, shipped to the customer |
| **Key partners** | Crizal (lenses); quality wholesalers (decorative items); basic-frame raw material suppliers |
| **Cost structure** | *Fixed:* factory, artisans, machines. *Variable:* raw materials, shipping (on-demand manufacturing) |
| **Revenue streams** | Asset sale (generic products), usage fee (via the recycling loop), advertising fee (social media now, television after a milestone) |

## ♻️ Recycling Loop

Need a frame for a particular look but don't need it afterwards? **Send it back.** EYECONIC recycles the material and gives you a portion of the value as **store credit points**. This reduces waste, supplies raw material, builds customer trust and encourages repeat purchases.
