## Slide I: Executive Summary

**Problem:** Lack of customization and creativity in eyewear.

**Solution:** A Bitmoji-inspired, AI-integrated home try-on filter that helps users find the exact eyewear they want, down to:
- Material
- Texture
- Colour
- Design
- Frame accessories: charms, chains and magnetic convertible brooches

**Target customer:** People who value accessibility and versatility, for example someone who needs:
- A specific accessory for a costume
- A picture-perfect frame for an office shoot
- A daily-wear look they have in mind

A dedicated **movies/dramas section** will let users get the exact frames worn by characters in their favourite movie or drama (e.g. K-drama fans).

**Core value proposition:**
- Convenience at a click.
- A **recycling loop**: unwanted frames can be sent back, the material is recycled, and the customer receives a portion back as **store credit points**.

## Slide II: Business Model Canvas

| Block | Summary |
|---|---|
| **Key partners** | Aim to be self-sufficient, but will start by sourcing raw materials for basic frames. Lenses from **Crizal** for quality; decorative items from quality wholesalers. Customised parts manufactured in-house. |
| **Key activities** | Make products through artisans and deliver directly to the customer with no middleman. The complete package is manufactured in EYECONIC and shipped to the customer's home. |
| **Key resources** | Crizal lenses; in-house frame manufacturing after an order is placed; artisans for assembly; shipping to customers. |
| **Value proposition** | Carefree, accessible, fast delivery. An eco-friendly recycling loop gives convenience, less wastage and trust in quality and management, and drives repeat customers. |
| **Customer relationships** | Stores offer many customisations for each customer's exact needs. The recycling loop builds trust in the brand's responsibility and liability. |
| **Channels** | After sourcing lenses and raw materials, products are assembled in the company's own factory by artisans and shipped directly to customers (**D2C**). |
| **Customer segments** | **Demographic:** any gender, any age; customizable frames at a pocket-friendly rate. **Geographic:** launch exclusively in India, with possible expansion later. **Occasions:** casual outings, formal meetings, weddings, fandom looks. |
| **Cost structure** | Cost-driven and affordable. *Fixed:* factory maintenance, artisans, machines. *Variable:* raw materials and shipping, with manufacturing on demand. |
| **Revenue streams** | **Asset sale:** generic products only, not custom ones. **Usage fee:** recycling returns money as store credit, so only a fraction of the purchase price is effectively spent. **Advertising fee:** promotions on social platforms, moving to television after a certain milestone. |
